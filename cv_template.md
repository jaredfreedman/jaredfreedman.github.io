# CV template — fill this out and send it back

## How your /cv/ page works
The Education, Work experience, Skills, and Service sections below are typed directly onto
the /cv/ page. Publications, Talks, and Teaching are different — each entry is its own file
in _publications/, _talks/, or _teaching/, and the page lists them automatically. Fill in
as many entries as you have; duplicate a block for each additional item.
