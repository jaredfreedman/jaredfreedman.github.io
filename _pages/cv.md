Education

* PhD in Integrative Biology, Oregon State University, Corvallis, OR, 2020 - 2025 (4.00 GPA)
* B.A. in Biology, Vassar College, Poughkeepsie, NY, 2014 - 2018 (3.56 GPA)

Research Experience
======
* September 2025 - Present: Postdoctoral Research Associate
  * Cornell University, Peter McIntyre Lab - Ithaca, NY
  * Conducting research using environmental DNA metabarcoding to survey biodiversity and phylogenetic patterns of cichlid fish in Lake Tanganyika, Tanzania, informing the development of protected area boundaries and management of aquaculture
  * Responsibilities include field sample collection, laboratory processing, bioinformatics and analysis, and writing of manuscripts and grant proposals
  * Cultivated working relationships with stakeholders at The Nature Conservancy and the Tanzanian Fisheries Research Institute, including obtaining research permits, planning and conducting field research, and generating reports to optimize the impact of the research

* September 2020 - June 2025: PhD Researcher
  * Oregon State University, David Lytle Lab - Corvallis, OR
  * Used eDNA metabarcoding to investigate spatial and environmental drivers of aquatic invertebrate community structure and diversity in the Lower Colorado River Basin, Arizona, USA
  * Used an eDNA metabarcoding approach to reveal patterns of landscape genetic connectivity in desert river networks in Grand Canyon, Arizona, USA
  * Established an effective eDNA metabarcoding workflow for current and future projects in the Lytle lab
  * Developed skills in eDNA sample collection, DNA extraction, amplicon PCR, digital PCR, Illumina sequencing, and fluency in R and command line languages for bioinformatic and analytical workflows

* June 2018 - July 2020: Research Technician
  * Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, Linda van Aelst Lab - Cold Spring Harbor, NY
  * Conducted research on the interaction of the proteins Unc5d and Dock7 and their role in cortical development using molecular biology techniques
  * Assisted other researchers with molecular and biochemical experiments for various neuroscience research projects
  * Managed the laboratory mouse colony, including perfusion, dissections, and breeding of transgenic mouse lines

* January 2017 - May 2018: Undergraduate Research Assistant
  * Vassar College, Justin Touchon Lab - Poughkeepsie, NY
  * Conducted independent research on gene expression patterns underlying phenotypic plasticity in treefrog tadpoles
  * Maintained frog and tadpole tanks, including feeding, cleaning, and monitoring health
  * Fellow, Vassar Undergraduate Summer Research Institute (May 2017 - August 2017): conducted research monitoring water-borne pathogens on Vassar's ecological preserve and studying the pathogen's effects on frog egg development

* January 2016; June 2016 - August 2016: Biology Intern
  * XTuit Pharmaceuticals - Waltham, MA
  * Assisted scientists in experiments in histology, immunohistochemistry, and fluorescence microscopy
  * Performed tissue slide staining and gene knockdown procedures
