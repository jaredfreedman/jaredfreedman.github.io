I'm Jared Freedman, a postdoctoral researcher using molecular ecology methods, namely environmental DNA (eDNA), to study freshwater biodiversity and conservation. I am primarily interested in using such methods to understand large and complex ecosystems, where the diversity of species and landscape heterogeneity makes conventional ecological methods impractical.

See my [Research Interests](/research-interests/) page for more on what I work on, my [Publications](/publications/), and my [CV](/cv/).
